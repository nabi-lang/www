# Brand

<div class="lead-sm">The Nabi name and mark, how to write and use them, and the files to download.</div>

## The name

Write **Nabi**, with a capital N, in prose and titles. Lowercase `nabi` is the command, and the name in code, package names, file names and addresses (`nabi run`, `nabi-lang.org`). Not NABI, not Nabi-lang. *Nabi* (나비) is Korean for butterfly.

The slogan is *Clear rules, free as a butterfly.* (in Korean: *규칙은 분명하게, 나비처럼 자유롭게.*).

## Using the name and the mark

The name and the mark identify the Nabi project, which [DaminStudio](https://daminstudio.com) maintains. You are welcome to use them to point at Nabi: in an article or a talk, on a page that links here, in a project built with Nabi or for Nabi (a package, an editor extension, a tutorial).

Please do not:

- suggest that your project, product or event is official, or endorsed by the Nabi project, when it is not;
- use the mark as the logo of your own project, or change its shape, proportions or colors;
- put the name in the name of a product or company in a way that could be taken for Nabi itself.

A package on the registry can be called anything that is not taken; the official ones are the packages published from the [nabi-lang](https://github.com/nabi-lang) organization. If you are unsure, ask at [contact@nabi-lang.org](mailto:contact@nabi-lang.org).

## The mark

The mark is a butterfly of folded triangles, in four pinks: `#93356A`, `#C9518A`, `#EC7FA9` and `#FBC6D9`. The wordmark sets *Nabi* beside it in Khand, in ink `#1F1B1E` on light grounds and in light letters on dark ones. Leave room around the mark of at least half its height, and do not set it on a background that hides its lighter wings.
